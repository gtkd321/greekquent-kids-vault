# Setting up

For grown-ups. Two minutes, once. Back to [[Today]].

> [!tip]- 1. Turn on the Greekquent tools
> The first time you open the vault, Obsidian asks whether you trust it. Tap **Trust author and enable plugins**. If you missed it: Settings, Community plugins, **Turn on community plugins**.

> [!tip]- 2. How a week works
> Each week has one game, the same week as the grown-up class. **Today** shows it as cards, top to bottom.
> 1. **This week's game:** what you need and how to play. Tap **Open the lesson** for the whole page.
> 2. **Say it together:** one new line a day. Say the earlier lines too. Sunday is the whole game. **One line at a time** shows each line big, with the voice.
> 3. **Now you are the teacher:** swap. The kid asks the questions, and the grown-up gets one wrong on purpose.
> 4. **This week's words:** tap a word to hear it, then pick a color.
> 5. **Do the moves:** a gesture for each phrase.
>
> Weeks with no class keep last week's game.

> [!tip]- 3. The buttons (Today, Words, Class, More)
> On a computer they sit in the thin strip of icons along the far left edge. On a phone, tap the menu button (three lines) at the bottom right.
> - **Today** (house): this week's game.
> - **Words** (list): the words, with a search box at the top for any word in English or Greek.
> - **Class** (two people): this week's lesson, homework and Ask the class.
> - **More** (three dots): Look up a word, Ask the class, Send homework, What's new, Update vault.
>
> Under the cards on Today: **Play Γιαγιά** (a word game with this week's words), **All lessons**, and **Look up a word**.

> [!tip]- 4. Colors
> Red is "not yet", yellow is "kind of", green is "got it". Tap the color after the kid tries the word from memory. Tap it again to clear it.

> [!tip]- 5. Your own notes
> Type in the **My Work** folder. Updates never change My Work.

> [!tip]- 6. Weekly updates
> When Obsidian says an update is ready, tap the message. Or tap More, then Update vault. Only changed files download, in the background.

> [!tip]- Works on
> Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, Mac and Linux, with the free Obsidian app. Nothing to buy.
