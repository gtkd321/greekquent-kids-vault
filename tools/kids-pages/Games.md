# Games

Back to [[Today]].

> [!summary]- Γιαγιά (find the notebooks, don't get fed)
> [Play Γιαγιά](obsidian://greekquent?open=giagia) (works offline)
> - Walk around the house and find 7 notebooks. Each one says a Greek word. Pick what it means.
> - Γιαγιά wants to feed you. If she catches you 3 times, it's dinner. Find all 7 and run out the door.
> - **Words from:** a kid lesson or all your words. On a word card, tap **More**, then **Play Γιαγιά with this word** to play that word and the words it goes with.
> - On a phone, use the arrows under the house or swipe. On a computer, use the arrow keys.

> [!summary]- Τι λέμε; (hello and goodbye)
> [Play Τι λέμε;](obsidian://greekquent?open=hello) (works offline)
> - Look at the sky and the clock. Are you coming in or going out? Pick what to say.
> - Coming in: Καλημέρα in the morning, Καλησπέρα after lunch. Going out: Καλό απόγευμα, Καλό βράδυ, or Καληνύχτα at bedtime.

> [!summary]- Τι βλέπεις στο ψυγείο; (what's in the fridge)
> [Play Τι βλέπεις στο ψυγείο;](obsidian://greekquent?open=fridge) (works offline)
> - Open the big fridge. Find the food: Πού είναι το γάλα; Then say what you see, and spot what the cat ate.

> [!summary]- Word web (tap, double-tap, hold)
> - Open a word card, tap **More**, then **Word web**.
> - Tap a word to hear it and move there. Double-tap a purple word to hear more than one. Hold a word to see what it goes with.

> [!summary]- Dessert word (a treat after the lesson)
> - At the bottom of [[Today]]. Pick a word, say it, make up a move, and draw it.
> - Tap **Add our own** to put your favorite words in the jar.

> [!summary]- Hello song
> - At the top of [[Today]]. Sing it together to start, and do the moves.
