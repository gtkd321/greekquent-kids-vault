# καλημέρα

**good morning** · say it: kaliméra

> Καλημέρα, Μαρία.

> [!tip]- When to say it
> Hello until about 1 pm, coming in. Leaving in the morning, say Καλή μέρα (have a good day) or Καλή συνέχεια.
> [Play Τι λέμε;](obsidian://greekquent?open=hello) to practice hello and goodbye through the day.

## Pair it with

Build with your words: take one from each line and say it out loud.

- **Use it with:** [[λέω]] καλημέρα (I say good morning)
