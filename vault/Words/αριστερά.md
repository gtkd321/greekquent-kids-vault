# αριστερά

**on the left, to the left** · say it: aristerá

> Στρίψε αριστερά.

## Pair it with

Build with your words: take one from each line and say it out loud.

- **Use it with:** γυρίζω αριστερά (I return left)

## Often appears with
- [[δεξιά]] — right (direction)
- [[παίρνω]] — I take
- [[μεγάλος]] — big, old (age)

**Common in news Greek**
