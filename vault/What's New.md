> [!tip]- 2026-11-19m · Class mode, and Greek words read letter by letter
> - **Class mode.** On the Class tab, tap **Start class mode** (or More, then Class mode). Class then shows big buttons for this week's **Script** and **Classroom Greek**, where each phrase is a card you tap to hear. A small bar on every page jumps between Phrases, Script and Class. The screen stays awake, and it turns off by itself after 2 hours.
> - **Greek read letter by letter?** That happens when a phone has no Greek voice, so it uses an English one. The app now stays quiet instead and shows how to add a free Greek voice. Find it any time under More, then Greek voice help.

> [!tip]- 2026-11-19l · The alphabet: sounds and names
> Today now has a **Letters** card from the first day, next to the story puzzle. Two tabs:
> - **Sounds:** drag each letter onto the sound it makes. Each letter comes with a word from the 1,500 (kids keep their picture words).
> - **Names:** say a letter's name, like άλφα, βήτα or γάμμα, and that letter fills in. You can say several at once. No microphone in the app? Tap **Use the keyboard** and use the keyboard's microphone, or type the name.
>
> Remembering a letter earns φόρα like a word, and each finished set earns 2. New students see it first under Next.

> [!tip]- 2026-11-19k · Comebacks listen to you
> Comebacks now have a **Say it** button, on Today and in Quiz me. Answer out loud in Greek and the phone checks you. When it hears your answer, the Greek shows, it counts as Had it and you get +2 φόρα for saying it. Close counts: about three words in four is enough on a long line. If your phone can't listen here, the button opens a box instead; tap the microphone on your keyboard and say it.

> [!tip]- 2026-11-19j · Android: Words tab and full screen
> Words is now always in the bottom bar, from the first day. Before, it appeared only after you colored your first word, so a new phone looked different. And if Chrome's Desktop site setting is on, which some Android phones and tablets do by default, the app used to look small with empty space around it. It now fills the screen either way.

> [!tip]- 2026-11-19i · Greek voice off
> On Android, the ringer switch silences calls and notifications but not the Greek voice. Now you can turn it off yourself: More, then **Greek voice: on (turn off)**. It stays off on this phone, games included, until you turn it back on the same way.

> [!tip]- 2026-11-19h · New game: Τι λέμε; (hello and goodbye)
> Greek greetings depend on the time of day and on whether you are coming or going. Καλησπέρα is only a hello; leaving, you say Καλό απόγευμα or Καλό βράδυ, and Καληνύχτα only at bedtime. The new game shows a sky, a clock and a scene, and you pick what to say, in three levels. Open it from Games, or from More on the καλημέρα, καλησπέρα and καληνύχτα word notes. Those notes also have a short When to say it box.

> [!tip]- 2026-11-19g · Leave out εσύ if you like
> In the story puzzle, Greek lets you drop εγώ, εσύ, εμείς or εσείς at the start of a sentence, because the verb already says who. Build it without the word and it counts; the line then shows it in parentheses, like (Εσύ) είσαι ο Νίκος; Each solved line now shows its φόρα: +1 the first time, +3 on a later day. A replay the same day says it was already counted.

> [!tip]- 2026-11-19f · The tour counts what you already did
> Picked a world, colored a word or played a game before the tour got there? It now counts: the step turns green, you get the 2 φόρα, and the tour moves on. This works for things done before you started the tour, too.

> [!tip]- 2026-11-19d · Α sounds like Α
> A capital vowel on its own, like Α! or Ε;, and short cries like Ωχ, Αχ and Ωπ, are now read aloud as sounds, not as "κεφαλαίο Α" or letter by letter. This works in every page and game.

> [!tip]- 2026-11-19c · Clearer phrases in the Word web
